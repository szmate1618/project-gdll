Bundled unmodified TrueType files in ui/fonts/plex/:
- IBMPlexSans-Regular.ttf: UI content, descriptions and standard controls.
- IBMPlexSansCondensed-Medium.ttf: modern titles, tabs and panel headings.
- IBMPlexMono-Regular.ttf: clocks, ammunition, coordinates and numeric readouts.

Source paths, file sizes and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in ui/fonts/README.md.
