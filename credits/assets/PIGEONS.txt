PIGEON FLOCK MODEL

"Animated Bird, Pigeon" by Paul Spooner (dudecon / Peripheral Arbor).
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/animated-bird-pigeon-797d27b68af3453e865149435df6aa30
Listed license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
The author also declares this work public domain: https://ip.tryop.com/

Changes: recolored with the supplied undead palette, reduced to a 16x16 atlas,
light feathers changed to RGB 165,161,152 (#A5A198), flight animation baked,
65cm wingspan, and reduced meshes prepared for distance-dependent detail.
Only the Flapping clip is used by the flock renderer.

Original FBX and texture were obtained from the publicly shared source archive:
https://github.com/orgs/assimp/discussions/5970
Source fingerprints and preparation details: assets/pigeon/sources.json and
assets/pigeon/README.md in the project source distribution.
