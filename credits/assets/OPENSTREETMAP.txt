OpenStreetMap supplies building footprints, roads, railways, fences, mapped
utility columns, guardrails, individual tree nodes, tree-row lines and forest
polygons. Tree rows are sampled into approximate tree placements; the default
OSM+tuned2 layer combines them with Sentinel-derived proposals. The project converts
these into approximate geometry and placements, with inferred dimensions and
local edits. These changes are by this project, not OpenStreetMap.
