VEGETATION DATA AND DERIVATION

Default preset: osm-tuned2.

OpenStreetMap contributors supply natural=tree nodes, natural=tree_row lines,
natural=wood and landuse=forest polygons. Local preparation samples tree rows
at up to 9 m intervals and supplies estimated dimensions where tags are absent.
The combined default retains the mapped nodes/row samples and adds tuned2
proposals, suppressing proposals within 6 m of those mapped positions.
See OPENSTREETMAP.txt for ODbL and attribution information.

Tuned2 uses native Sentinel-2 RGB pixels, Astra forest colour statistics and a
saved colour/context classifier. OSM forest pixels are excluded from detection.
The Astra forest alternative is traced from Sentinel imagery. Both are modified
Sentinel-derived data. Their actual reference acquisition dates and layer hashes
are recorded in the generated VEGETATION.txt notice; see SENTINEL.txt for terms.
The raster, classification profiles and geographic input databases are not
included merely because their provenance is recorded in a runtime JSON.

Tree positions, row spacing, tree dimensions and forest boundaries are
approximations, not surveyed trunk positions, measured crowns or current inventories.

OPTIONAL ORTHOPHOTO PRESET / CALIBRATION PROVENANCE

The orthophoto preset uses original tree-detector CSV positions from 2016
orthophotos and manually reviewed dense-canopy cores, constrained by 2025
satellite outlines. The local source records contain tile filenames, EOV CRS
overrides and fingerprints, but do not establish a provider identity, license
or permission to redistribute the resulting data. Do not interpret this notice
as permission. Provider and applicable acquisition terms still require review.
The default asset pack excludes these original placement files and polygons.

The tuned2 recall/precision models were calibrated using colour statistics at
missed original reference trees and reviewed negative regions. Inference uses
Sentinel colour/context features rather than copying original coordinates or
orthophoto pixels. The provenance of those calibration references should remain
part of a distribution review; this notice does not make a legal determination
about rights in learned statistics or their resulting predictions.
