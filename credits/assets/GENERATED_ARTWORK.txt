PROJECT-GENERATED ARTWORK AND GEOMETRY

Tree and pine impostors:
  assets/tree_impostor.glb and assets/pine_impostor.glb embed four foliage PNG
  views each. The source images were generated with the built-in imagegen tool
  and locally processed; prompts and calibration are documented in assets/README.md
  and the tree/pine .prompt.txt files. They approximate foliage volume and are
  not views of a measured or externally licensed tree model.

Forest volume textures:
  assets/forests/side.png and top.png were generated with imagegen. The saved
  prompts and repetition scale are documented in assets/forests/README.md.
  assets/forests/shrub.png is an imagegen-generated transparent side/top atlas
  for runtime old-forest shrubs. Its generation and layout-edit prompts are
  saved in assets/forests/shrub.prompt.txt.

Railway texture:
  assets/railways/track.png is generated deterministically by this project's
  Python script using Pillow/NumPy, then embedded in the town GLB. No source
  photograph is used; see assets/railways/README.md.

Sample scene, inferred town geometry, pole columns and guardrail geometry:
  Geometry is built by the project. Mapped footprints, alignments and placements
  retain OpenStreetMap attribution. Terrain imagery/elevation retain the separate
  Sentinel and Copernicus DEM notices. Project-created geometry does not remove
  rights or obligations attached to its source datasets.

Weapon models and zombies have separate third-party provenance and terms;
see WEAPONS.txt and ZOMBIES.txt. Audio is conditional; see AUDIO.txt.

This file records provenance. It does not claim exclusivity in generated
imagery, grant rights in unrelated material, or replace any provider's license.
