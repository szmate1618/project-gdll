COPERNICUS SENTINEL-2 IMAGERY

The town's terrain imagery is derived from Copernicus Sentinel-2 Level-2A
true-color imagery. It is cropped, resampled, and mapped onto terrain.
MAP.txt in the matching asset pack supplies the required modified-data
notice with the actual acquisition year from the published map metadata:

Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data [Year]

[Year] above describes the notice format; use the completed notice in MAP.txt.
The year is the imagery acquisition year, not the game release/download year.
