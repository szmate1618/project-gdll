White-vest open-prism zombie: LOD4 distance survey, 2026-10-29

Decision
--------
The 60-triangle / 60-vertex Male_C proxy is a plausible geometry candidate at
160-180 m in the default 1280x720 / 60-degree vertical-FOV camera. It meets the
exploratory original-reference geometry budgets at every sampled elevation from
160 through 240 m. This does not establish a clean LOD3-to-LOD4 switch: no tested
distance from 80 through 320 m meets the switch budgets across all elevations.
Keep the runtime policy unchanged until the silhouette/coverage mismatch between
current LOD3 and the proxy is addressed and the transition is checked again.

At 180 m the proxy's median supersampled area is 115.1% of original, while
current LOD3 is 71.8%. Median native color coverage is respectively 116.7% and
75.0% of original. The paired proxy/LOD3 native coverage ratio has median 1.571.
This proxy preserves more limbs but visibly broadens the figure at the switch.
Increasing distance is not a clean fix: native raster dropout appears beyond
200 m. Default gameplay draw cutoff is 180 m, so farther results require an
extended/unlimited draw distance. These +/-15-degree views do not validate
steep overview/top-down views, where open ends can matter more.

Torso color
-----------
The torso now uses source-derived off-white sRGB (208,205,201), linear RGB
(0.630757,0.610496,0.584078): the median of 110 brighter neutral mid-torso
texture samples. It reads as a light vest instead of the previous pink/brown
corner sampling. Only the six torso COLOR_0 rows changed. Positions, normals,
joints, weights, indices, remaining 54 color rows, rig and animation are identical.
The previous prototype is retained. Geometry remains ten uncapped triangular
columns, 60 shared vertices, 60 triangles, 20 open rims / 60 boundary edges.

This is a mostly-white torso approximation, not a faithful torn-vest color map.
The original also contains exposed neck/back/waist skin and garment openings.
The new all-white column does not reduce aggregate RGB error against original:
192 matched smoke views at 180 m give median foreground RGB RMSE 0.124 -> 0.152
and p95 0.213 -> 0.235. At 12 m the medians are 0.162 -> 0.167. The source vest
color and visual intent are retained; color-region fitting still needs work.
These byte-space sRGB metrics are descriptive, not perceptual acceptance scores.

Protocol fixed before capture analysis
--------------------------------------
One character: ZombieMale_C.glb. References: original 1650 triangles / 3402
vertices and current LOD3 114 triangles / 141 vertices. Proxy: 60 / 60, saving
47.4% triangles and 57.4% vertices versus this character's current LOD3.
Those counts do not establish an FPS gain or an all-ten-character result.

10 distances: 80,100,120,140,160,180,200,240,280,320 m.
8 yaw angles: 0,45,...315 degrees.
8 cycle phases: 0,1/8,...7/8 of idle,walk,run.
3 camera elevations: -15,0,+15 degrees.
4 native pixel offsets: (0,0),(.5,0),(0,.5),(.5,.5).
23,040 matched views; three variants -> 69,120 renders and 138,240 PNGs.
The analyzer makes three pairwise comparisons per view: 69,120 metric rows.

Primary comparison: prism versus current LOD3. Secondary comparisons: each
versus original. Exploratory budgets, declared before analysis:
- Across-view p95 of per-view symmetric silhouette-boundary p95 <=0.5 pixels.
- Across-view p99 of per-view maximum silhouette-boundary error <=1 pixel.
- No view where reference has native color pixels but candidate has none.
A stable tested threshold requires every sampled elevation and every farther
tested distance to meet all budgets. No primary threshold passed. Color/shading
changes are reported separately and are not certified by these geometry limits.

Camera is normal 1280x720, 60-degree vertical FOV, no AA; original idle height
normalized to 1.8 m. Eye rotates about aim point (0,1.65,0), with distance measured
to that point. Production LOD instead measures actor-bound-center distance.
All captures use the nominal-distance stepped frame, 5 Hz throughout this sweep;
80 m is therefore a forced LOD3 reference at every elevation. All variants share
the exact displayed baked frame. No continuous movement or simulation is timed.

64x64 integer-aligned color crops at x=608,y=336 retain native scene pixels.
Separate depth silhouettes use 4x linear resolution; boundary sampling is thus
quantized to 0.25 native pixels. The same native projection offset is used for
both resolutions. Normal production VAT shaders/default sunlight, authored
sRGB source textures, no scene geometry/fog/shadows/postprocessing. GPU:
AMD Radeon 660M, radeonsi, Mesa 24.2.8, hardware OpenGL4.6.

At 180 m, no source, LOD3 or proxy view has zero native color pixels. At 240 m,

Projection and model caveats
----------------------------
The trial range is conditional on this one character and native 720p scene.
A native 1080p scene has 1.5x the projected pixel height at equal distance;
approximately 1.5x distance gives the same pixel size, but was not rendered here.
A 720p scene upscaled into a 1080p window retains 720p scene sampling.
A same-pose A/B blink illustrates switching; eight poses per clip are a survey,
not continuous animation or a camera-approach sequence. No human perception
study or frame-time timing was performed. Runtime LODs and assets are unchanged.

the 4x masks by exactly two pixels; 18 far-versus-near crop color/mask pairs are
identical. Original production manifest fingerprint is unchanged.

native pixels and duplicate rejection. Metadata completeness guard passes real
captures and rejects a removed whole matched group and wrong sample count.

compilation and git diff --check passed. PNG comparisons and blink frames were
