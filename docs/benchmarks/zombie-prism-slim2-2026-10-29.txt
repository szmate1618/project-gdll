Prism Slim 2: narrower and lower upper rear torso corners, 2026-10-29
===================================================================

Decision
--------
Keep Slim 1 as the stronger overall fit for this character at 80 m. Slim 2
implements the requested edit and is retained in both colorings, but introduces
a small aggregate regression and can expose a gap below the head in some poses.
Both remain 60 vertices / 60 triangles with ten uncapped triangular columns.
No production model, LOD threshold or runtime policy is changed.

Original-relative silhouette IoU median falls from Slim 1 0.755572 to Slim 2
0.749610. Slim 2 is better in 616/2304 views (26.7%), worse in 1668 (72.4%), tied
in 20. The standard prism was 0.727463 and current LOD3 is 0.700485, so Slim 2 is
still better on this aggregate than those earlier geometries. Area/original
moves from 1.014628 to 0.985959; similar total area does not imply better overlap.
Median edge p95 stays 0.500px. Across-view p95 slightly worsens 0.705996->0.707107;
maximum-edge across-view p99 stays 1.744934px.

Matched-LOD2 IoU median falls 0.734645->0.729672, across-view edge p95 grows
0.600112->0.649722px. Including the 10 Hz predecessor to 5 Hz successor step,
IoU median falls 0.717741->0.709926, with edge-p95 across-view p95 changing
1.235154->1.239113px. Both LOD2 comparisons improve in 664 views, worsen in 1620,
and tie in 20. The narrowing/lowering is not an improvement in overall switching
quality. All variants remain visible at native resolution in every sampled view.

Exact geometry edit
-------------------
Slim 2 starts from Slim 1: original torso transverse scale 0.85 and terminal
hand/foot-end scale 0.7 remain. Only upper-rear torso POSITION rows 52/53 change.
Their separation becomes 85% of Slim 1 about the pair's midpoint, then both are
translated down the torso axis by 10% of its pre-change fitted axial height.
Torso height is 67.707049cm in source bind units, giving a 6.770705cm shift along
axis. The source faces +Z: front apex 51 remains unchanged. The other 58 positions,
all vertex colors, joints, weights, indices, rig and animation bytes are retained.
Only normals are recomputed for the changed geometry.

Metadata records the edited indices, original/new pair midpoints, axis, height,
scale and translation. The upper rim is now tilted; its stored fitted center is
explicitly labeled a pre-edit reference, and the actual vertex centroid is also
recorded. No cap, extra vertex or neck piece is added.

The API accepts profile='standard'|'slim'|'slim2', color_mode='sampled'|'white_vest';
defaults and legacy slim=True remain compatible. CLI --profile slim2 combines
with --color sampled or --color white-vest. Old standard and Slim 1 GLB bytes
still reproduce their four historical fingerprints exactly.

Color and angle results
-----------------------
Both colors are frozen from the matching Slim 1 variant. Original-reference
common-support median RGB RMSE grows from sampled 0.133610 to 0.134500 and from
white 0.134506 to 0.137000. White Slim 2 has higher error in 1492/2304 views (64.8%).
Sampled-color changes improve many small cases but include a few larger losses;
its mean error grows by 0.001033. Against the 10 Hz LOD2 predecessor, white grows
0.141611->0.143394 and sampled 0.154793->0.154525; metrics are mixed, not a global
color improvement. Slim 2's original-mask-only medians are sampled 0.144617 and
white 0.148705 versus Slim 1's 0.143985 and 0.145269.

Original-relative IoU medians regress in every clip:
  idle: 0.76427->0.75871
  walk: 0.75564->0.74720
  run: 0.73491->0.73064
All three elevation medians regress. At +15 degrees, 644/768 views worsen;
at -15 degrees, 448/768 worsen. Side yaw 90 improves mean IoU by 0.00242, while
270 degrees is nearly tied. Front yaw 0 loses 284/288 views (mean delta -0.01437);
rear yaw 180 loses 260/288 (mean delta -0.01706). The loss is concentrated around
upper torso coverage rather than a uniform benefit from a smaller footprint.
Original native-color missing-fraction p95 increases 0.240000->0.259259.

Neck-gap diagnostic
-------------------
A visually verified example is 12 m, walk phase 0.125, yaw 180 degrees, level camera,
zero pixel offset: source and Slim 1 retain projected head/body connection at
supersampled depth, while Slim 2 shows background below the head. The comparative
PNG is slim2-neck-gap-comparison.png. This is a rendering gap from the requested
upper rear-edge edit; no neck geometry was added to compensate.

A separate 8-connected 4x-depth-mask diagnostic counts whether the component
containing the topmost foreground pixel differs from the largest-area foreground
component. At 80 m it triggers in original 0/2304, Slim 1 32/2304 and Slim 2 372/2304;
340 are newly separated and none restored. This aggregate is a top-component
diagnostic, not a verified anatomical label for every view. Raw per-view flags
and its definition are saved in top-component-diagnostic.csv/json.

Protocol and metrics
--------------------
One ZombieMale_C character, native 1280x720 and 60-degree vertical FOV, nominal
80 m eye-to-aim distance about (0,1.65,0). Eight yaw angles, eight phases per each
idle/walk/run clip, elevations -15/0/+15 degrees, four native subpixel offsets.
2304 matched views x 10 variants = 23040 renders, each native RGB plus 4x linear
depth mask: 46080 PNGs. The analyzer produces 31 pair comparisons/view = 71424
metric rows, 124 pooled/elevation summaries, plus angle/clip and paired successor
improvement tables. References are original, matched 5 Hz LOD2 and forced 10 Hz LOD2;
all other variants use the same nominal 80 m 5 Hz displayed frame. This samples a
rate transition; it is not continuous animation or a camera-approach test.

Variants: original, LOD2, LOD2_10Hz, current LOD3, both standard prism colors,
both Slim 1 colors and both Slim 2 colors. A separate 12 m inspection contains
source and both Slim 1/Slim 2 colors, eight phases/yaws and three clips, level/no-jitter:
960 renders, with matched 15 Hz frames. The renderer uses hardware AMD Radeon 660M,
normal VAT shader/default sunlight, no scene/fog/shadows/AA/postprocessing.
The capture target was successfully rebuilt before rendering.

IoUmed is median silhouette overlap; higher is better. AreaRatio is median
candidate/reference supersampled area (target 1). Edge95med is the median of
per-view symmetric boundary p95. Edge95p95 is across-view p95 of those per-view
values. Max99 is across-view p99 of each view's maximum edge error. All edge
units are native scene pixels. RGBcommonMed is median RGB RMSE/255 on identical
per-view support shared by all ten variants. The old eight-variant report used
a different support; compare candidates within this report. Original-mask-only
errors are retained separately. No image metric is a human acceptance threshold.

API compatibility, Slim 1 geometry invariants, exact two-rear-corner Slim 2 edit,
and all profiles' fixed colors/rig/weights/open topology. Independent geometry
audit confirms exactly 52/53 changed, other 58 byte-identical, requested separation
and translation, unit normals, 60 vertices/60 triangles/20 open rims/zero caps.
