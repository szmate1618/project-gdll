Prism Slim: smaller torso and terminal ends, 2026-10-29
======================================================

Result
------
Prefer the slim geometry over the previous standard prism for the next 80 m
LOD3 integration trial on this character. Both requested colors are saved as
new models; standard controls remain intact. Slim preserves 60 vertices and
60 triangles, with ten open columns and no caps. Runtime policy is unchanged.

At 80 m, original-reference median silhouette IoU improves from standard 0.7275
to slim 0.7556 (current LOD3 0.7005). Median area/original improves from 1.1582
to 1.0146. Median edge p95 improves 0.559 to 0.500 native pixels; across-view
p95 improves 0.7906 to 0.7060. Max-edge across-view p99 is nearly unchanged,
1.750 to 1.745 pixels (current LOD3 2.609). Slim has better original-relative
IoU in 2052/2304 matched views (89.1%), worse in 252. Every yaw and elevation
median improves. Absolute area error improves in 2068 views (89.8%).

Against matched 5 Hz LOD2, IoU improves from 0.6922 to 0.7346; slim is better
in 2256/2304 views (97.9%). Against the 10 Hz LOD2 predecessor, IoU improves
0.6768 to 0.7177; slim is better in 2108/2304 views (91.5%). Current LOD3 remains
slightly closer to LOD2 in median IoU (0.7303 including rate change), so the
replacement improves source fidelity while retaining a transition tradeoff.

Geometry requested
------------------
Torso: both rings' transverse offsets about their fitted centers scale by 0.85,
reducing width/depth diameters 15%; axial positions and torso height stay fixed.
Hand/foot ends: only the distal lower-arm/lower-leg ring vertices scale by 0.7
about their wrist/ankle bind joints, including terminal overhang. Elbow/knee
rings, upper limbs and head stay fixed. Exactly 18 vertex positions change;
42 remain identical. The original two-ring columns become more tapered toward
the smaller tips; there are no separate hand/foot meshes to resize independently.

Both colorings preserve their corresponding standard COLOR_0 arrays exactly.
Normals are recomputed after geometry changes. Bone joints/weights, indices,
original rig/animation document and full source binary prefix remain unchanged.
The standard CLI/API paths still reproduce both historical GLBs byte-for-byte.

Color results
-------------
Within the new eight-variant report, median common-support RGB RMSE against
original is standard sampled 0.1368, standard white 0.1436, slim sampled 0.1336,
slim white 0.1345, current LOD3 0.1425. Slim improves over its standard same-color
control in 74.0% of sampled-color views and 80.2% of white-vest views.

The two slim colors are closer in aggregate than the standard colors. Earlier
sampled colors have lower original-reference RGB error in 1237/2304 views (53.7%);
white wins 1067 (46.3%). Median error differs by only about 0.0009. The white vest
still has a larger light region than the torn source garment, particularly at
the back; it gives a smaller color jump from LOD2 than the sampled-color proxy.
Against the 10 Hz predecessor, common-support medians are slim white 0.1416,
slim sampled 0.1548, current LOD3 0.1391. These byte-space RGB errors describe
images, not human perception or a guaranteed visual ranking.

A residual cost of narrowing: original native-color missing-fraction p95 grows
from standard 0.1765 to slim 0.2400, still below current LOD3 0.3935. Some thin
pixels disappear even as overall shape improves. All eight variants remain
visible in every sampled native view; paired colorings have identical depth
masks for both standard and slim geometries.

Vertex colors and animation
---------------------------
Appearance uses glTF COLOR_0 vertex colors sampled from the source texture
at preparation time. The off-white version assigns the torso's six vertices
one source-derived sRGB color (208,205,201). The prism material has no base-color
texture. Original source images remain in the retained GLB binary but are unused
by that primitive. Animation still uses the normal shared VAT texture buffers.

These Male_C loops retain their complete approximately 30 Hz baked data:
  idle: 220 frames / 7.3333 s
  walk: 64 frames / 2.1333 s
  run: 58 frames / 1.9333 s
LOD3 selects poses on its 5 Hz grid; the survey samples 8 cycle phases per clip.
No separate texture-versus-vertex-color timing or crowd performance measurement
is part of this experiment. Geometry counts do not imply the same percentage
frame-time gain.

Capture protocol
----------------
One ZombieMale_C character, native 1280x720 with 60-degree vertical FOV, nominal
80 m eye-to-aim distance about (0,1.65,0). Eight yaw angles, eight phases per
idle/walk/run, three elevations (-15,0,+15 degrees), four native raster offsets.
2304 matched views x 8 variants = 18432 renders, each with native color and 4x
linear depth mask: 36864 PNGs. The analyzer makes 22 pair comparisons per view:
50688 metric rows, 88 pooled/elevation summaries, plus yaw summaries and paired
standard-to-slim improvement counts. All candidates use matched 5 Hz frames;
a separate forced 10 Hz LOD2 reference captures the predecessor's sampled rate.

A separate 12 m inspection captures original and all four prism variants at level
zero-jitter camera, all 8 yaws/8 phases/3 clips:960 renders with 15 Hz stepping. This
closeup is for checking geometry and appearance, not an 80 m transition test.

Capture target was successfully rebuilt before both hardware runs. Normal VAT
shader/default sunlight, no world geometry/fog/shadows/AA/postprocessing. GPU:
AMD Radeon 660M, radeonsi/rembrandt, hardware OpenGL 4.6/Mesa 24.2.8. The survey is
not continuous motion, a human acceptance study or steep top-down validation.

Metrics (pooled 2304 views per row)
---------------------------------
IoUmed: median original/reference silhouette overlap; larger is better.
AreaRatio: median candidate/reference supersampled area; target 1.
Edge95med: median of per-view symmetric boundary p95, native pixels.
Edge95p95: across-view p95 of each view's boundary p95, native pixels.
Max99: across-view p99 of maximum symmetric boundary error, native pixels.
RGBcommonMed: median RGB RMSE/255 on identical per-view support shared by all 8
variants (union of their supersampled masks reduced to touched native pixels).
Color support differs from the earlier six-variant report, so use within-report
comparisons. Original-mask-only errors are also retained in the raw summaries.

Verification
------------
Three focused Python regressions pass: historical/default reproduction, exact
requested torso/tip changes with fixed proximal parts, and preserved color/rig/
weights/open topology. Independent geometry audit confirms 18/42 changed/fixed
positions, requested scale factors, normalized normals and exact regeneration.

Both GPU captures pass GL-error, foreground and crop-border guards. The 80 m
camera Cartesian product is complete; all 36864 unique color/mask files exist;
inputs, shaders and manifests match hashes. All 27648 color/mask files for the
six previous control variants are byte-identical to the previous 80 m experiment.
Slim sampled/white depth masks match in all 2304 views. Original production
manifest fingerprint is unchanged; no prior artifact was overwritten.

Analyzer synthetic six/eight-variant CLIs pass known IoU/color metrics, reject
partial slim pairs/depth mismatch/missing groups, and preserve old six-variant
metrics on a retained real view. New preview, boundary and 12 m closeup PNGs were
inspected. Python compilation, scoped diff review and git diff --check pass.
No runtime policy update, global character generation, performance claim was made.
