Random-location eight-hour sleep simulation, 2026-11-06
==========================================================

50 outdoor trials and 50 first-floor trials; first floor means one level
above ground. Sleep starts at approximately 22:00 and targets 06:00.
All durations below are in-game HH:MM:SS, not real elapsed time.

Ten outdoor and nine first-floor trials ended immediately because a travel
horde member was already within the wake radius. These locations were retained;
the random sample did not require an initial safe distance from actors.

All five first-floor noise wake-ups were zombies physically hitting doors.
Trace replay confirmed the zombie-contact acoustic profile and door category.
The outdoor noise wake-up was a zombie attack-body collision; zombie damage
also occurred in that tick, but the noise wake was processed first. Each trial
is counted once using the actual first wake reason.

The one first-floor damage wake was reserved interior zombie 53002 at 00:02:55.2,
after 02:02:50 asleep, consistent with the upstairs midnight activation policy.
No first-floor trial was interrupted by a dog.

Why upstairs players still wake
------------------------------
Travel-horde proximity does not require a zombie to enter the building.
Noise propagation currently uses distance without wall/floor attenuation.
Closed doors can prevent contact while door impacts still wake the player.

Method
------
Current published map: output/godollo.glb.
Scenario: scenarios/gray-scott-corridors/scenario.toml, population seed 42.
53,000 outdoor zombies (including 1,000 travelers), 10,000 reserved interior
slots, 500 dogs, and the usual 1,000 parked cars. Birds disabled.
Location-selection seed: 20261106. Start hour 22; day length 1200 simulation
seconds. Production World::step at fixed 1/60 s ticks; no wall-time pacing.
Eight game hours correspond to 400 simulation seconds (24,000 ticks).
The input startup buffer adds approximately 4.8 game seconds before sleep.

Outdoor: uniform random X/Z over the map bounds, rejecting missing terrain,
building footprints, steep/unwalkable support, or blocked player capsules.
No actor-clearance filter. These are map-area samples, not road-only samples.

First floor: shuffle distinct mapped building identities, retaining the first
50 whose production floorplan has a valid first-floor room center. Select a
random valid first-floor room center within each building. 209 buildings were
examined. Buildings without supported walkable upper floors are ineligible.
Production lazy floorplans were loaded, then exterior doors were closed before
the first tick. Interior doors remained closed. Normal interior encounters ran.
Players were directly placed on verified floor support because normal spawning
prefers outdoor terrain. Final upstairs displacement never exceeded 0.0121 m.

Each trial ran in a serial Linux copy-on-write child from the same untouched
loaded scene. Actor movement, doors, injury, encounters and time from one trial
cannot carry into another. World construction resets the simulation clock.
No map/assets or production gameplay sources were changed for the experiment.

Observation-only scratch copies of world.cpp, zombie_scenario_routes.cpp and
physics_impacts.cpp logged state transitions, attackers, travel actors and
acoustic sources. The six noise trials were replayed with more detailed physics
tracing: all six exactly matched original positions, wake reasons, times and
health. Results count only the original 100 trials.

Validation and limits
---------------------
Successful rebuild: cmake --build build --target test_sleep -j4.
Focused test: ctest --test-dir build -R ^multiplayer_sleep$ --output-on-failure
(passed). Checked 100 completed rows, 50 per cohort, exact six-trial replay
agreement and unchanged source/map hashes during the experiment.
This is one seeded sample of one scenario/population, with different outdoor
and building sampling rules. It measures current accelerated-sleep behavior,
including approximate traveler movement; it is not a full-fidelity movement
comparison or a probability estimate for every map/scenario.
