Zombie LOD3 quality and 100k rendering controls — 2026-10-28
=========================================================

Decision
--------
Keep the current 7%-of-original LOD3 globally. The 5% candidate reduces mean
triangles from 125.8 to 89.2 (29.1%), but loses additional limb/head coverage
at its 80 m entry distance and sometimes contributes no native color at 180 m.
The 3% and 2% candidates degrade more. A 5% farther-distance option would be
an explicit quality tradeoff, not an appearance-preserving replacement.
Production geometry, lods.json and runtime LOD thresholds were not changed.

The 10% control is a finer comparison mesh, averaging 183.9 triangles. It shows
detail already lost by current LOD3. It is not a production setting change.
A hand-authored silhouette/deformation-focused mesh is a promising next
experiment; no custom mesh was authored in this comparison.

Edge distances are native scene pixels relative to the original mesh. First
compute per-view p95 symmetric nearest silhouette-boundary distance, then
summarize those values across views. Boundaries include gaps/holes. This is a
sampled image metric, not a vertex error bound or human perception threshold.

Native coverage is the median ratio of visibly colored pixel counts to the
original. Pixels differ by more than one RGB byte from the flat background.
It is separate from supersampled depth coverage: small figures can have valid
depth at subpixel sample positions while contributing no native color pixels.
At 180 m this happens for candidate 5% in 2/1,920 views, candidate 3% in 4 views
and candidate 2% in 6 views. Original and current LOD3 have no such cases.
One verified 5% case is Female_B, walk phase 0.25, yaw 135 degrees, 180 m:
all 4,096 native image pixels equal the background RGB (31,38,48), while the
original has three non-background pixels and current LOD3 has two.

The first-character idle heights are 14.00, 9.25 and 6.25 native pixels at
80/120/180 m: 1.8 m height, 720p and 60-degree vertical FOV. Projection gives
about 9.4 pixels at 180 m at native 1080p.
Compared directly with current LOD3, candidate 5% edge-p95 medians at the three
depths are 0.354/0.250/0.250 pixels; across-view p95 is 0.750/0.512/0.405.
The modest aggregate added error includes visibly missing limbs in some views.

100,000-zombie rendering control
-------------------------------
Four sequential standard zombies-open-100k-v1 processes in current/5%/5%/current
order. Same rebuilt executable, production shaders, hardware and settings; only
the scratch manifest's LOD3 geometry changes. All 100,000 actors are submitted
in ten draws, with source/rig/LOD1/LOD2 fingerprints preserved. Camera triangles
change from 12,580,000 to 8,920,000. No zombie shadow triangles are submitted
in this overview; sun shadows retain the normal 1024-square map.

Each process runs six rounds, each with 32 warmup and 64 measured frames on the
same deterministic 60 Hz timeline. GPU queries remain asynchronous. Startup,
query draining and presentation are outside render timers. The observer keeps
the behavior pool idle; this is not 100,000 active pursuers.

Run       Mesh       CPU render ms  GPU elapsed ms  GPU frame p95 ms
0         Current           18.987          14.609             21.787

2         5%                16.338          12.916             18.052

CPU/GPU columns use median of six round means; GPU frame p95 uses all measured
frames. Median of two process results: CPU render 18.731 -> 16.475 ms (-12.0%).
CPU render includes driver waits; this does not isolate CPU instruction cost.
GPU time is inconclusive for a stable percentage improvement: the baseline
before/after drift (2.295 ms) exceeds the difference between variant medians
(1.383 ms), and the second candidate is slower than the final baseline.
Clocks, temperatures, power and unrelated system activity were not controlled.
Our captures, analysis and builds did not run concurrently with these timings.
CPU and GPU overlap; do not sum them or translate them to presented FPS.

Repeated screenshots are byte-identical within each variant. Between current
and 5% captures, 4,226 native pixels differ. This is a visual/performance tradeoff,
not an optimization with identical output. The overview was inspected and
shows the crowd across the field.

Verification and reproduction
------------------------------
Hardware: Radeon 660M, radeonsi/rembrandt, LLVM 19.1.1, DRM 3.61,
Linux 6.11.0-29-generic; OpenGL 4.6 Core, Mesa 24.2.8-1ubuntu1~24.10.1.
Both focused capture and standard crowd targets built successfully. All quality
and crowd runs passed their GL, population, pose, empty-mask and capture guards.
All 80,640 quality color/mask files exist. Production lods.json retains its hash.

The quality capture reads distances from its manifest and uses the public LOD
policy for displayed frame cadence (5 Hz here). The 64-square crop at full-scene
top-left (608,336) preserves the normal 1280x720 projection and pixel alignment.
Depth masks use four-times linear sampling. Default sunlight/materials are fixed;
scene geometry, shadows, blending, dithering, fog and postprocessing are absent.
Distances are forward depth; 180 m deliberately bypasses camera distance culling.

Tight-mask analysis exactly matches prior full-mask measurements on retained
near captures, including every old first-asset summary field. Quarter-pixel
fixtures, near/far manifest compatibility, Python/script syntax and whitespace
checks passed. Pose cycling is not continuous playback or a transition test.
PNGs were inspected directly; no local-file browser-policy workaround was used.

Use docs/BENCHMARKS.md, adding --family far during preparation and choosing a
separate output directory. Runtime assets stay local. Ignored evidence:
build/benchmarks/zombie-lod3-quality-20261028/.
assets/manifest.json and shaders/ identify quality inputs; captures/metadata.json,
samples.csv and analysis/ retain images and metrics. setup.json records hashes.
crowd-assets/ retains contained validated scratch assets (source/sidecar symlinks
and contained geometry hard links). crowd-run-0-* through crowd-run-3-* retain
raw CSVs, metadata, captures and summaries; crowd-summary.json records run order,
summaries and screenshot checksums.
