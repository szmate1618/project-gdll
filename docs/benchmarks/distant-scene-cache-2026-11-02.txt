Distant scenery cache experiment — 2026-11-02

Branch: experiment/distant-scene-cache; base revision 44494d1.

Result
------
The feature remains opt-in. The final clear distant overview reduced GPU cost
22.1% while increasing CPU rendering cost 41.6%. Refresh spikes remain. This
is useful evidence for a distant-background experiment, not a general gameplay
FPS improvement. Nearby scenery, animated actors and shadows retain their
normal rendering cadence.

The first version also cached an empty background in the open 100,000-zombie
control, adding about 1.28 ms of total GPU overhead (6.504 -> 7.785 ms). This
was total cache-path overhead, not an isolated measurement of the composite
shader. The final version bypasses cubemap allocation, patches and composition
when the current view contains no eligible distant static geometry. The crowd
control now reports distant_cache_active=0 and all images remain identical.

Method
------
AMD Radeon 660M, OpenGL 4.6 Core, Mesa 24.2.8-1ubuntu1~24.10.1.
1280x720; ordinary sunlight shadows; nearest vegetation/cached texels.
1,000 m nearest conservative 3D-bounds cutoff; 15 Hz refresh; 256-pixel angular
cubemap tiles. Normal-view faces are 1280x1280 (approximately 75 MiB color/depth).
Scope views bypass the cache. Complete batches crossing the cutoff stay near.

Map cases alternate disabled/enabled rounds in one process: 3 rounds per mode,
32 warmup and 128 measured frames per round. All 18,432 GPU measurements exist.
The explicit presentation clock and camera motion advance exactly 1/60 second
per frame. Walking turns use 1.5 m/s and 40 degrees/s; fast turns use 180
degrees/s. No AI, dynamic physics steps, audio, HUD or presentation runs here.
Queries are asynchronous; max four frames in flight; waits and captures remain
outside CPU timers. Firefox was closed for the final runs. GPU workloads ran
sequentially, without concurrent builds or rendering tests.

Values below are milliseconds, median of per-round means. p95 pools measured
frames. GPU and CPU overlap: do not add their costs or convert either directly
to gameplay FPS. GPU clocks, power and temperatures were not pinned. Absolute
baseline GPU times shifted substantially between earlier and final runs; use
within-run comparisons. See also docs/benchmarks/cpu-gpu-overlap-2026-10-22.txt.

Refresh and turn patches
------------------------

distant-overview-clear-fast-turn / patch | 132 | 3.790 | 1.974
distant-overview-clear-fast-turn / refresh | 96 | 6.639 | 7.844

distant-overview-clear-static / refresh | 96 | 6.890 | 8.871
distant-overview-clear-static / reuse | 288 | 3.395 | 1.756
town-static / refresh | 96 | 10.170 | 12.340
town-static / reuse | 288 | 5.384 | 2.998

For the stationary overview, 96 of 384 frames refreshed and 288 reused the
cache. At 180 degrees/s, 132 frames patched newly exposed directions without
a full refresh, 156 reused all visible directions, and 96 refreshed. This
confirms that turning does not automatically trigger a complete redraw.
Refresh frames still perform several spatial queries and repeat geometry
submission across angular tiles; near terrain also uses partitioned index
ranges. These are added CPU/draw costs. The timing run does not isolate each
cost or establish that composite arithmetic alone causes the spikes.

Open 100,000-zombie control
--------------------------
Final runs use off-a, on-a, on-b, off-b order; each block contains the standard
6 rounds of 32 warmup / 64 measured frames. Combined: 768 frames per mode,
100,000 visible living instances, 10 draws, 6,000,000 camera triangles every
measured frame; same lowest prepared LoD. Static scene: one 1,200 m ground
surface. Idle AI updates run; dynamic physics/audio/player movement do not.

Mode | GPU | CPU render | GPU p95 | CPU render p95
off | 9.414 | 11.995 | 14.425 | 15.655
on | 8.793 | 11.063 | 12.784 | 14.826

Both modes use direct rendering in this case after the bypass fix. The final
samples show no persistent earlier GPU penalty. Do not interpret the residual
off/on timing difference as a cache speedup: there is no cached work here,
and block means vary. All four final captures are pixel-identical.

Image checks and validation
---------------------------
21 of 24 matched map capture pairs are pixel-identical. Only the three clear
distant overview pairs differ: mean absolute RGB error 1.22–1.31 on a 0–255
scale; 1.67–1.98% of pixels have any channel differing by more than 20. The
inspected overview/fast-turn comparison shows distant silhouette/texture
sampling differences, with no visible unfilled tile strips. The final captures
are byte-identical to those inspected before the empty-view guard was added.

Full default CMake build passed after the final changes. Before the final
bypass, the complete C++ suite passed 218/219 tests. multiplayer_game_menu
timed out at 90 seconds twice, after reaching its final hosted session/window
close step, with the cache disabled. No menu fix was attempted in this task.
After the bypass, all four rebuilt focused rendering regressions passed:
distant scenery, tree billboards, forest visibility and building rendering.
The new regression covers yaw/face boundaries, looking up/down, depth
occlusion, turn patches/reuse, refresh cadence, independent frozen-animation
clocks, teleport/resize/scope handling, and near-only <-> distant transitions.
A one-second hidden client run with --distant-scene-cache also completed.
