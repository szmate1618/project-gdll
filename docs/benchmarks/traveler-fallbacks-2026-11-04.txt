Corridor traveler fallback investigation, 2026-11-04
==================================================

Finding
-------
The increasing scenario-travel cost reproduces without dogs or rendering.
Repeated crowd corrections cause distant travelers to leave reduced movement
and use the full solver, including navigation/steering, throughout a 0.25-second
cooldown. Each fallback produces approximately fifteen additional cooldown
updates. Late in the run, 49 fallback entries/step correspond to 791 full-solver
calls/step, rather than just 49 expensive calls.

Measured minute means, ms per simulation step unless labeled as counts:

83% of the last minute's coverage time occurs inside the full solver. Its
inclusive time accounts for approximately 91% of the increase in total update
time. These are nested measurements, not additive stage totals.

Last-minute fallback causes:
  Crowd correction                     30.557/step, approximately 62.0%
  Conservative scene clearance         12.486/step, approximately 25.3%
  Zero requested velocity               4.294/step, approximately  8.7%
  Intermediate-step navmesh coverage    1.492/step, approximately  3.0%
  Target ground support                  .461/step, approximately   .9%
  Intermediate support                  negligible
  Planned endpoint navmesh coverage     zero

The crowd branch triggers on any change to the proposed X/Z movement, including
stops and slides. It does not mean every correction requires a new path search;
the benchmark performs no runtime path searches. It currently invalidates the
reduced segment and selects full navigation plus collision movement anyway.

Scene rejection is also conservative: 10.743 of its 12.486 events/step reject a
nonvertical surface, .673 a potential door region, and 1.071 a trunk. Wall and
candidate-capacity rejections are negligible or zero. This is not evidence of
12.486 actual blocking collisions. A focused 20% grade (about 11 degrees) slope
regression reproduces surface rejection while the full solver walks normally
at 1.2 m/s with exact terrain support.

Suggested next change
---------------------
Separate distant navigation/steering cadence from the full physical collision
solver. Crowd interference should not automatically require recomputing the
route follower at 60 Hz for the entire cooldown. Keep body/crowd collision,
ground support and final navmesh guards at their required cadence. Repeated
immutable-mesh coverage queries from stationary actors are another candidate
for exact-result reuse. Neither optimization is implemented in this investigation.

Setup and limits
----------------
Release C++17, AMD Ryzen 5 7535HS, Linux, pinned to CPU 0. Current source starts
at commit 59ea04d; unrelated cloth changes in the workspace were preserved.
Scenario: scenarios/gray-scott-corridors/scenario.toml, seed 42.
53,000 authored zombies, including 1,000 travelers, plus 10,000 dormant reserved
interior slots; zero dogs. Observer X/Z (-700,300), eye Y 6.99757 m.
1,157,182 collision triangles and 155,825 trunks, with doors and static prop poses.
No physics advancement, networking, audio or rendering. Fixed 1/60 s updates,
60 warmup steps followed by 72,000 measured steps in one detailed round.
Tables compare the first and last 3,600-step windows. Compilation and tests did
not run during the measured long run. Profiling overhead is included.

The published map changed since the previous cache benchmark:
  SHA256 97c1af98601cc01a0351e0ba1eb128330e026f3823481a1ae3ab24c293ea1030
The former map was 1c9efcf7e71beb3c67ca2cfbc77231f5c70de62781faa3bc185ce8617c6e560d.
This investigation reproduces the aging pattern, not the user's exact gameplay
route, active gameplay crowd or GPU/CPU contention. Its absolute timings are
not a direct comparison with that capture or the earlier map's benchmarks.

Verification and diagnostics
----------------------------
Movement policy is unchanged. New counters separate fallback causes, full
solver invocation types and scene-clearance rejection types. Fallback entries
are now counted before calling the solver, including calls whose combined
movement may subsequently be rejected; previously that rare case was omitted.

A short current-map run matches the saved pre-instrumentation executable's
final poses byte for byte. Two current-code control/detailed rounds also match.
Four focused CTest suites pass: traveler, navigation, movement and collision.
Tests cover wall versus conservative slope rejection, cooldown accounting,
profiling equivalence, float corners and dynamic geometry. The viewer and
benchmark targets build successfully. The long run is one detailed round;
it is not itself an unprofiled-control trajectory comparison.

Reproduction
------------
cmake --build build --target benchmark_zombie_updates -j4
taskset -c 0 ./build/multiplayer/benchmark_zombie_updates output/godollo.glb \
  scenarios/gray-scott-corridors/scenario.toml build/benchmarks/traveler-long \
  -700 300 --steps 72000 --rounds 1
