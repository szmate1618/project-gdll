Real-map zombie update substep profiling, 2026-10-23 (Europe/Budapest)

Source and workload
Base revision e8c6be4 plus the working-tree scenario-motion cache optimizations
and opt-in zombie substep profiler. Release (-O3), Linux, CPU-only measurements.
Current scenarios/test-horde/scenario.toml: seed 42; 1,000 roamers + 600 crossing.
Shared scene loader: 1,160,022 static collision triangles, 155,825 tree trunks.
Stationary player: X=-526.465, Z=296.095; eye Y=0.129849.

Method
Headless tests/benchmark_zombie_updates.cpp; fixed dt=1/60. Three detailed and
three control runs, ordered control/detailed/detailed/control/control/detailed.
Reload the seeded population and preplan all paths before every run. Discard
60 warmup steps; retain 300 steps/run, 900 rows in each mode. Scene loading,
planning, file output, rendering, networking, audio and physics advancement are
outside the measured update. Contact queries still read live props/door poses.
Perception, crowd collision, locomotion and spatial updates remain enabled.

The host uses HPET for its OS clock, with roughly 1.2 us per steady_clock read.
Fine scopes therefore use a serialized invariant TSC calibrated against the OS
clock while remaining on one CPU. Calibration and queries guard against CPU
migration. Final runs were pinned to allowed CPU 0; no samples were omitted.
Calibration: 3.03627e-10 seconds/counter tick. These are elapsed wall times, not
CPU instruction counts; scheduling and host clock/power variation can affect them.

Interpretation
Contact queries and navmesh coverage dominate the named stages, together about
64% of the instrumented update. Ground lookup, Detour selection and spatial
updates are individually much smaller in this workload. The remaining work
includes position lookup, waypoint following, nearby-trunk gathering, physical
constraint bookkeeping, crowd queries, locomotion, behavior dispatch and timer
bookkeeping. This report does not separately attribute that remainder.

Contact timing includes the full CollisionWorld::contacts query: static-triangle
BVH traversal and geometry tests, trunks, doors/boxes, allocation and sorting.
Coverage includes Mesh::segmentClear, including its endpoint polygon lookups.
Ground includes cached/fallback support queries and live-box support checks.
Steering measures Controller::steer; nearby-trunk gathering belongs to the
remainder. Spatial timing includes perception/crowd index updates and shot bounds.

All six runs produced exactly matching final transforms, locomotion clocks and
walking/running flags for every zombie. Path request count stayed at the 1,600
startup requests: zero runtime path searches. Focused navigation, scenario and
movement CTest suites passed after the final profiler change. Viewer builds.

contain perturbed or invalid-clock experiments and are not the reported results.
