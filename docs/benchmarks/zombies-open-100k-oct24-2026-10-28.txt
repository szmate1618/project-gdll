Open 100k: October 24 build versus current — 2026-10-28
======================================================

Result
------
The October 24 build is not materially faster in this comparison. Median of
two process results: CPU render 21.932 ms historical versus 21.897 ms current;
GPU elapsed 17.119 ms versus 16.974 ms. Differences are smaller than observed
within-version variation. This experiment does not reproduce a substantial
rendering slowdown between October 24 and current HEAD.

Protocol
--------
Unmodified tracked zombies-open-100k-v1: 1280x720, ten actual characters with
three prepared LODs, six rounds per invocation, 32 warmup and 64 measured frames
per round. Idle behavior uses an observer outside activation range and a fixed
60 Hz timeline. Distance removal is disabled. All 100,000 actors are submitted
at LOD3: 10 draws and 12,580,000 camera triangles, no zombie shadow casters.
Normal shadows use 1024-square sun maps; fog is disabled. There are no audio,
player movement, dynamic physics, birds or vegetation workloads.

Hardware: Radeon 660M, radeonsi/rembrandt, LLVM 19.1.1, DRM 3.61,
Linux 6.11.0-29-generic, Mesa 24.2.8-1ubuntu1~24.10.1, OpenGL 4.6 Core.
No power/hardware settings changed; GPU performance level remained auto.
Runs were sequential, without concurrent builds. The runner checked for other
benchmark/build processes, paused five seconds, then waited for three consecutive
0% GPU-activity samples at 0.5-second intervals before every invocation.
Read-only activity, clock-state, temperature and load samples were retained.
GPU temperatures ranged from 53 to 67 C. Idle samples do not establish matched
clocks or absence of all background GPU activity during timed rendering.
