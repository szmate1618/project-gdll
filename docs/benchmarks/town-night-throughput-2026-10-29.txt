Predeclared estimator and decision rule
---------------------------------------
Average elapsed wall time over each 512-frame block. Pair adjacent comparison/
radix blocks, normalize as comparison-minus-radix ms/frame, take the median of
four pair differences per process, then median across four processes. Positive
values mean radix saves time. Flat levels independently take the median of four
blocks per sorter/process, then median across processes. Percent paired saving
uses the aggregate comparison flat level as denominator.

Before measurement, a clear gain/regression required all three conditions:
1. At least 1% median paired change in the corresponding direction.
2. All four process paired medians with that sign.
3. At least 12 of 16 adjacent pairs with that sign.

This is an operational practical threshold, not a significance/equivalence test.
The regression action was to suggest reverting only the town sorter. Mixed or
below-threshold outcomes required judgment; they were not automatically called
equal or safe. Here conditions 1 and 2 favor radix, but condition 3 is 11/16.

One context/Renderer per process renders the same actual-map night-town-on
camera at 1280x720, with normal nearest vegetation, original haze/ground fog,
100 m old-forest policy and held flashlight. Sunlight and flashlight shadow
casting are inactive in this case. No AI, audio, player updates, simulation or
presentation runs. Animation input follows the same deterministic 60 Hz cycle.
Map/tree/forest and camera fingerprints are the retained inputs used by the
preceding controls. No generation or asset changes occur in this experiment.

Each block performs untimed ordered-ID/count/capture checks, then 128 warmup
renderer.render+glFlush calls and an untimed glFinish. Measurement is exactly:
read CLOCK_MONOTONIC; repeat 512 renderer.render+glFlush calls; glFinish; read
CLOCK_MONOTONIC again. The final GPU retirement wait is inside elapsed time.
Captures, hashes, counters, events and output writing stay outside the interval.
There are no per-frame clocks, GPU timestamp queries or explicit frame fences.

Queue policy differs from the preceding timestamp probes: glFlush submits every
frame, while the driver provides implicit backpressure. No four-frame pending-
query cap is imposed. This measures completed offscreen render throughput with
CPU/GPU overlap, not presentation FPS, input latency or general gameplay FPS.
Its absolute frame times are not interchangeable with earlier GPU intervals.

Each process has eight balanced blocks (4,096 measured frames). ABBA expands to
comparison/radix/radix/comparison/radix/comparison/comparison/radix; BAAB reverses
the sorter names. Four processes use ABBA, BAAB, BAAB, ABBA. Total measured work
is 16,384 frames. Before each process the runner checks known competing work,
waits five seconds and requires three zero-GPU-activity checks 0.5 seconds apart.
It collects only before/after snapshots and blocks while the child runs: no
sampling thread, event polling, clock/memory reads or other local analysis runs
during the child. No power or clock settings are changed.
