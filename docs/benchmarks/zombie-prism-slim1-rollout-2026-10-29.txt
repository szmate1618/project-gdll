Result and active assets
------------------------
All ten zombie types now use Slim 1 for LOD3: 60 triangles / 60 shared vertices
per type, starting at the existing normal-FOV 80 m threshold with 5 Hz poses.
LOD1/2 entries and all 20 geometry files remain byte-identical. Source GLBs,
rigs, animations, collision/severed assets and the 180 m draw cutoff are unchanged.

Geometry, colors and renderer correctness
----------------------------------------
Slim1 uses ten open triangular columns. Torso diameters are 15% smaller than
standard; only terminal hand/foot rings shrink 30% about wrist/ankle anchors.
Female D omits unused right finger bones in its skin; fitting now tolerates
those optional bones while requiring actual limb start/end joints.

Color comes from source-derived linear vertex colors. Male C uses its verified
off-white vest; the other nine retain sampled authored colors. The coarse
endpoint interpolation can miss light clothing patches (notably Female D/E),
which is a retained appearance approximation rather than an exact texture copy.
Both female/male 80 m contact sheets were visually inspected. All 5760 image-quality
renders passed GL errors, nonempty masks and crop checks. This is 5760 renders,
1920 matched original/previousLOD3/newLOD3 views across all 10 types, 8 yaws, 8 phases
and idle/walk/run at 80 m. Native 720p, 60-degree projection; 4x linear depth masks.

Fitted prisms can extend beyond original mesh bounds. Renderer upload now unions
baked animation and bind bounds across all loaded LODs before initializing
instance/live-pose bounds. Mesh-local bind units remain separate from animated
scene meters; original physics placement is unchanged. A focused GPU regression
keeps outlying LOD geometry visible when the original is outside the frustum,
compares culling with unculled pixels, and checks the same case with a live pose.
