Pine tree impostor texture set

All four images were generated with the built-in imagegen tool. The side and horizontal
slices use the generated front view as their appearance reference. Outputs are
1024x1024 RGBA PNG files. Existing source transparency was preserved apart from tiny
alpha specks in empty space. Painted neutral backgrounds were removed locally using
the user's previously approved background cleanup, with edge-color decontamination
and premultiplied-alpha resizing.

Files and placement:
pine.png: front elevation, trunk base near bottom center.
pine-side.png: approximate right-side elevation, 90 degrees around the vertical trunk.
pine-top-mid.png: horizontal slice at 0.5 of full tree height from trunk base.
pine-top-two-thirds.png: horizontal slice at 2/3 of full tree height from trunk base.

Use equal world-space square extents for the two horizontal slices. Their foreground
widths are about 480px and 385px, respectively, based on corresponding crown widths
in the generated front view. Their approximate trunk pivot is image center (0.5,0.5).
The project is Y-up: horizontal planes are XZ, top camera looks along -Y, +X is image
right and +Z is image bottom. Heights are measured upward from the visible trunk base.
The set is visually matched generated artwork, not exact projections or geometric
cross-sections of a shared 3D model. Branch placement and depth are approximate.

pine.png generation prompt:
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: full-tree frontal orthographic texture for a 3D pine-tree impostor, 1024x1024 pixels, transparent PNG.
Primary request: one realistic upright pine tree on a truly transparent background. A healthy medium-height woodland pine with a single straight trunk, reddish gray-brown scaly bark, naturally irregular tapering conical crown, open tiers of spreading woody boughs, and dense small tufts of long forest-green pine needles. Recognizably a pine rather than a broadleaf tree or a perfectly manicured Christmas tree. Fine photographic needle and bark detail. Some gaps between the branching tiers and a visible lower trunk extending about one fifth of the tree's full height below the lowest living branches. A few asymmetrical branch tips make the tree natural. No snow or decorations.
Composition: front elevation, camera level and orthographic, tree completely upright. Whole tree visible from root flare to highest needle, with generous transparent margins and no cropped branches. Exactly square 1024x1024 canvas, central trunk axis x=512, crown tip y=40, trunk base y=984, maximum crown width about 650px. This establishes the identity and framing for matching side and horizontal-slice textures.
Lighting: soft diffuse neutral daylight, even natural color, realistic detail, no strong directional highlights or baked cast shadow.
Background: genuine transparent alpha background around the tree and in gaps through the branches. No landscape, ground plane, grass, sky, checkerboard graphic, text, labels, watermark or other objects.
Output one 1024x1024 RGBA PNG.
