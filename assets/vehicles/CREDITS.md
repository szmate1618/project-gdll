# Vehicle asset credits

All listed assets are supplied under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The following attribution text is retained from the official downloaded archives.
Current listing author/profile names are additionally recorded in `sources.json`.
Keep these notices and the modification notice with redistributed assets.

Modification notice: source glTF files were packed into self-contained GLBs.
Variants were separated, unused binary data was removed, and selected meshes were
reduced with meshoptimizer 1.3 as recorded in `preparation.json`. The Swift selected
output also joins identical solid-material parts and bakes their transforms.
Original source archives and their notices are retained without modification.
