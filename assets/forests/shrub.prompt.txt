Historical generation prompts for the shrub atlas, preserved verbatim.

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, with transparent_background=true.
Final layout uses the second image, generated by editing the first.

Initial generation prompt:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: transparent game foliage sprite atlas for an OpenGL bush billboard. Create ONE square 2048x2048 RGBA image divided into TWO equal square cells side by side (left half and right half), absolutely no dividers or text. LEFT HALF: a single isolated low wide dense deciduous woodland bush, straight orthographic SIDE elevation, width exactly four times its visible height, centered in its half with generous fully transparent padding. Dense small muted natural summer green leaves, rounded irregular organic silhouette, some dark twig structure, no tall trunk. RIGHT HALF: the SAME bush seen in orthographic TOP DOWN view as a roughly circular leafy canopy, centered entirely inside the right half with transparent padding on all sides. Both views have soft neutral diffuse illumination, natural texture detail, matching dark green palette. Both subjects must be fully contained within their respective half. Transparent background including between leaves and around silhouettes. No ground, no grass, no shadows cast on any background, no sky, no scenery, no labels, no border. The two halves will be mapped separately to crossed upright side cards and one horizontal card at half the bush height.

Final layout-edit prompt:

Edit this game bush atlas to enforce exact layout. Preserve the realistic natural leafy bush appearance and transparent background. The canvas must be SQUARE. The LEFT half of the canvas is one square cell, containing ONLY a straight orthographic side view of a very low wide bush, VISIBLE WIDTH 4 TIMES VISIBLE HEIGHT. Fit this side bush entirely between x=5% and x=45% of the whole canvas, centered vertically at y=50%. The RIGHT half of the canvas is one square cell containing ONLY the top-down orthographic circular view of the same leafy bush, entirely between x=55% and x=95% of the whole canvas, centered vertically at y=50%. There must be a completely empty TRANSPARENT vertical strip from x=45% to x=55% spanning full height. No part of either bush may cross this strip or enter the other half of the image. Both views must be entirely inside their own cell and not touch canvas edges. Keep natural muted summer green leaves, soft neutral lighting, organic silhouette and real transparency around subjects. No text, labels, guides, grid, frame, ground, or cast shadows. Square 2048 by 2048.
